# Engineering verification and Magadan field pilot / design draft

All candidate sites require consent of operator and lawful data access. No site-specific physical model has been verified as of 2026-10-20.

## Six module dossiers

1. **HydroForecast — Kolyma basin candidate**: source provenance, hydropost, meteorology, snow, temporal cutoff, ensemble uncertainty. Compare MAE/CRPS/reliability against a customer-approved baseline. No claim to operational water management.
2. **CascadeEconomics — Kolyma + Ust-Srednekan cascade candidates**: certified reservoir tables, hydraulic connectivity, rulebooks and validity periods, efficiency curves, unit availability, no forbidden discharges. Validate mass balances, power curves and stress constraints. Use shadow-mode only.
3. **TurbineReliability — candidate 9 hydrogenerators**: engineering-approved fault/event ontology, regime conditioning, diagnosis of confirmed defects; no failure probability without calibration data. Report false alarms per operating time and recall on confirmed cases.
4. **GridLoss Planner — candidate Magadan grid section**: verified topology, clock-aligned meters, net balance, technical loss solver validated vs electrical engineering tool, normalized demand. Unexplained balance is not proof of fraud.
5. **HeatNet Economics — candidate Magadan TPP/heat network section**: valid thermal/hydraulic scheme, calibrated resistances and temperatures, time/ambient normalization, certified comparison vs incumbent software. Avoid control-room connection.
6. **HydroCAPEX — demonstration portfolio**: reconcile technical work orders/engineering conclusions with actual corporate investment system; cost sources, cashflow scenarios, mandatory safety category, procurement and northern logistics, actual results after works.

## Evidence tiers

- S0 — synthetic, only software correctness.
- S1 — open licensed data with source validation.
- S2 — authorized historical operator data, reproducible backtest.
- S3 — independent engineering validation and named responsible expert.
- S4 — shadow-mode observation under operator-approved safe boundary.
- S5 — production readiness subject to information security, support, architecture, compliance and owner approval.

No S0-S2 result can be called a safety-approved operational recommendation. Per module, keep an objective stopping criterion: refusal of lawful data, unsuitable source quality, lack of incremental value over already-deployed incumbent, unverified physical model, or excessive cost of deployment.

## Comparative acceptance matrix

For every existing tool (e.g., ZuluThermo, RastrWin3, enterprise budgeting or EAM) define same data snapshot, same technical constraints, same decision question, independently reviewed accuracy and end-to-end cost. Publish both strengths and weaknesses, not just selected wins.

## Northern site engineering

Check legal permissions and rights to assets, hydrometeorological assumptions, permafrost presence based on geotechnical investigations (not assumed), seismic rating for precise site, logistics, access windows, earthworks, system operator requirements, and environmental/social constraints. Construction budgets only after route/survey/quantity take-off; demonstration NPV not a verified construction estimate.
