## Required G0-G8 staged governance

G0 sponsor and data owner, legal permission, permissions matrix; G1 data quality and classifying sensitive infrastructure data; G2 engineering physical baselines; G3 independent numerical reference and declared tolerances; G4 design/safety permissibility under valid operating rules; G5 financial authorization and separate confirmation of own benefit registry; G6 construction/permitting path established for actual interventions; G7 commissioning with evidence; G8 post-implementation measured savings and deviations with external factors isolated.

Each gate needs: an accountable legal organization, named authorized reviewer, versioned source document, measurement protocol, date, refusal and remediation workflow. Not passing a gate blocks higher status regardless of dashboard presentation.

## Mandatory engineering scope distinctions

A hydrological forecast is NOT a water use permission. A technical anomaly is NOT a proven failure prediction. A balancing residual is NOT proof of electricity theft. A heat-balance residual is NOT automatically a leak. A synthetic NPV is NOT a tariff or a grant application approval. A corporate lab login is NOT field access to industrial control systems.

## Magadan-specific screening before estimates

Collect site-specific geotechnical/permafrost evidence, climate and seismic design inputs, haulage routes and seasonal accessibility, equipment procurement windows, restrictions of river basin and land-use permissions, and outage windows. None may be inferred from city-level averages. Source every quantity and price; classify unknowns as missing. Use competent designers and actual permits for any project moving beyond screening.
